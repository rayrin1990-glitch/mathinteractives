Variable Replacement Logic

Understanding why shifting right uses minus in the algebra.

Original: \( y = x^2 \)
New: \( y = (x - 2)^2 \)

Since the point (x - 2, y) must satisfy the original rule y = x², we replace the original \(x\) with \((x - 2)\):

\( y = (x - 2)^2 \)
