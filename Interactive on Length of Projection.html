The Concept of Direction

Projection is not about which formula you remember.
Projection is about direction.
The Central Question:
“How much of vector a lies in the direction of vector b?”
To measure length in a particular direction, we need a unit ruler in that direction. Because we are projecting onto b, the ruler must be the unit vector b̂.
The Math:
Length = | a · b̂ |
(Where b̂ = b / |b|)
