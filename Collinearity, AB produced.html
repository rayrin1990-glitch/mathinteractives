What does "Produced" mean?

In geometry, producing a line means extending a line segment in a specific direction:

  • AB produced: Start at A, go through B, and keep going. Point C lies on this if it's "beyond" B.
  • BA produced: Start at B, go through A, and keep going. Point C lies on this if it's "beyond" A.
